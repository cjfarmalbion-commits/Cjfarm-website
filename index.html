What We’re About

CJ Farm is a small Pennsylvania farm with a big heart. Our goal is to rescue and care for animals while creating a welcoming place where people can connect with animals, nature, and one another.

❤️

Animal Rescue

Providing rescued animals with a safe, caring place to live and thrive.

🐐

Animal Education

Helping visitors learn about farm animals, responsible care, and the relationships we build with them.

🤝

Community

Working toward a farm experience that welcomes veterans, people with special needs, families, and our community.
