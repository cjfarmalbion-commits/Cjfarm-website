What We Do

CJ Farm is a small Pennsylvania farm dedicated to rescuing and raising animals while creating a welcoming, peaceful farm experience for veterans, people with special needs, families, and animal lovers.

🐐

Animal Rescue

Providing rescued and unwanted farm animals with a safe, loving place to call home.

❤️

Community

Creating opportunities for people to connect with animals, nature, and each other.

🇺🇸

Veterans & Special Needs

Working toward a farm experience where veterans and people with special needs can visit, relax, and enjoy the animals.

🌱

Growing Together

Building CJ Farm one animal, one visitor, and one act of kindness at a time.
